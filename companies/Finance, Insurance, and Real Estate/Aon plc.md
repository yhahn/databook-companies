Aon plc, as it files with the SEC (CIK 0000315293): its fiscal years and quarters from its XBRL filings, FY2007 to FY2026 (FY2026 through Q2). Dollars are in millions; EPS in dollars a share. Capex and buybacks are cash paid out, so negative. EPS diluted is as filed, a share as it was when filed; EPS adjusted is a share as it is now, each split since divided out. A quarter the filings give only as a running total is the total less the quarters before it; Q4 is the year less the first three.

**How large is Aon plc, and how has it grown?** It sold $17.2 billion in FY2025, the 204th most of the 487 companies with a revenue for the year, and the 31st of 93 in [Finance, Insurance, and Real Estate](../Finance,%20Insurance,%20and%20Real%20Estate.md). Its sales rose 81.2% from FY2015 to FY2025, 6.1% a year.

**Does Aon plc make money, and how much of what it sells does it keep?** It earned $3.7 billion in FY2025, 21.5% of what it sold; the median company of its sector kept 21.5%. It lost money in none of its 19 years in the book.

**Where does Aon plc's cash go?** From FY2016 to FY2025 its operations brought in $24.7 billion; it spent 8.1% of that on plant and equipment, and 14.8% on buying back its own shares.
