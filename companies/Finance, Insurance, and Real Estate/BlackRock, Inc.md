BlackRock, Inc., as it files with the SEC (CIK 0002012383): its fiscal years and quarters from its XBRL filings, FY2016 to FY2026 (FY2026 through Q2). Dollars are in millions; EPS in dollars a share. Capex and buybacks are cash paid out, so negative. EPS diluted is as filed, a share as it was when filed; EPS adjusted is a share as it is now, each split since divided out. A quarter the filings give only as a running total is the total less the quarters before it; Q4 is the year less the first three. The SEC's list gives BLK to CIK 0002012383; its filings before are under CIK 0001364742, and are taken from there, each filing from the CIK that filed it: BlackRock, Inc. (CIK 0002012383, formerly BlackRock Funding, Inc.) became the parent of the old BlackRock in October 2024, when it bought Global Infrastructure Partners, and took the ticker BLK; the old BlackRock's filings, to its 10-Q of 2024-08-06, are under CIK 0001364742 (named BlackRock Finance, Inc. since; the SEC's submissions).

**How large is BlackRock, Inc., and how has it grown?** It sold $24.2 billion in FY2025, the 151st most of the 488 companies with a revenue for the year, and the 23rd of 93 in [Finance, Insurance, and Real Estate](../Finance,%20Insurance,%20and%20Real%20Estate.md). Its sales rose 97.5% from FY2016 to FY2025, 7.9% a year.

**Does BlackRock, Inc. make money, and how much of what it sells does it keep?** It earned $5.6 billion in FY2025, 22.9% of what it sold; the median company of its sector kept 21.5%. It lost money in none of its 10 years in the book.

**Where does BlackRock, Inc.'s cash go?** From FY2016 to FY2025 its operations brought in $38.8 billion; it spent 7.2% of that on plant and equipment, and 42% on buying back its own shares.
