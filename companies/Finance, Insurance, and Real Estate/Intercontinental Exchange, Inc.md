EPS diluted is as filed, a share as it was when filed (before 2017-02-07, a share before a split: see the changes); EPS adjusted is a share as it is now, each split since divided out. A quarter the filings give only as a running total is the total less the quarters before it; Q4 is the year less the first three.

**How large is Intercontinental Exchange, Inc., and how has it grown?** It sold $12.6 billion in FY2025, the 257th most of the 488 companies with a revenue for the year, and the 39th of 93 in [Finance, Insurance, and Real Estate](../Finance,%20Insurance,%20and%20Real%20Estate.md). Its sales rose 170% from FY2015 to FY2025, 10.4% a year.

**Does Intercontinental Exchange, Inc. make money, and how much of what it sells does it keep?** It earned $3.3 billion in FY2025, 26.2% of what it sold; the median company of its sector kept 21.5%. It lost money in none of its 15 years in the book.

**Where does Intercontinental Exchange, Inc.'s cash go?** From FY2016 to FY2025 its operations brought in $31.8 billion; it spent 7.3% of that on plant and equipment, and 22.3% on buying back its own shares.
