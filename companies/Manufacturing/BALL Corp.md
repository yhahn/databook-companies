EPS diluted is as filed, a share as it was when filed (before 2011-08-09 and 2017-08-09, a share before a split: see the changes); EPS adjusted is a share as it is now, each split since divided out. A quarter the filings give only as a running total is the total less the quarters before it; Q4 is the year less the first three.

**How large is BALL Corp, and how has it grown?** It sold $13.2 billion in FY2025, the 253rd most of the 488 companies with a revenue for the year, and the 102nd of 202 in [Manufacturing](../Manufacturing.md). Its sales rose 64.6% from FY2015 to FY2025, 5.1% a year.

**Does BALL Corp make money, and how much of what it sells does it keep?** It earned $915 million in FY2025, 7% of what it sold; the median company of its sector kept 11.7%. It lost money in none of its 18 years in the book.

**Where does BALL Corp's cash go?** From FY2016 to FY2025 its operations brought in $11.5 billion; it spent 78.7% of that on plant and equipment, and 55.5% on buying back its own shares.
