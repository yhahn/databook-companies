**How large is Merck & Co., Inc., and how has it grown?** It sold $65.0 billion in FY2025, the 57th most of the 488 companies with a revenue for the year, and the 21st of 202 in [Manufacturing](../Manufacturing.md). Its sales rose 63.3% from FY2016 to FY2025, 5.6% a year.

**Where does Merck & Co., Inc.'s cash go?** From FY2016 to FY2025 its operations brought in $134.6 billion; it spent 25.3% of that on plant and equipment, and 23.2% on buying back its own shares.
