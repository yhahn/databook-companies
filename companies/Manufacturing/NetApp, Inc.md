NetApp, Inc., as it files with the SEC (CIK 0001002047): its fiscal years and quarters from its XBRL filings, FY2009 to FY2027 (FY2027 through Q1). Dollars are in millions; EPS in dollars a share. Capex and buybacks are cash paid out, so negative. EPS diluted is as filed, a share as it was when filed; EPS adjusted is a share as it is now, each split since divided out. A quarter the filings give only as a running total is the total less the quarters before it; Q4 is the year less the first three.

**How large is NetApp, Inc., and how has it grown?** It sold $6.6 billion in FY2025, the 353rd most of the 487 companies with a revenue for the year, and the 153rd of 202 in [Manufacturing](../Manufacturing.md). Its sales rose 7.3% from FY2015 to FY2025, 0.7% a year.

**Does NetApp, Inc. make money, and how much of what it sells does it keep?** It earned $1.2 billion in FY2025, 18% of what it sold; the median company of its sector kept 11.8%. It lost money in none of its 18 years in the book.

**Where does NetApp, Inc.'s cash go?** From FY2016 to FY2025 its operations brought in $12.7 billion; it spent 13.6% of that on plant and equipment, and 75.8% on buying back its own shares.
