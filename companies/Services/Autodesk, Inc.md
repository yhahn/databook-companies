Capex and buybacks are cash paid out, so negative. EPS diluted is as filed, a share as it was when filed; EPS adjusted is a share as it is now, each split since divided out. A quarter the filings give only as a running total is the total less the quarters before it; Q4 is the year less the first three.

**How large is Autodesk, Inc., and how has it grown?** It sold $6.1 billion in FY2025, the 362nd most of the 488 companies with a revenue for the year, and the 45th of 75 in [Services](../Services.md). Its sales rose 144% from FY2015 to FY2025, 9.3% a year.

**Does Autodesk, Inc. make money, and how much of what it sells does it keep?** It earned $1.1 billion in FY2025, 18.1% of what it sold; the median company of its sector kept 16.3%. It lost money in 4 of its 19 years in the book (FY2016, FY2017, FY2018, FY2019).

**Where does Autodesk, Inc.'s cash go?** From FY2016 to FY2025 its operations brought in $10.3 billion; it spent 3.7% of that on plant and equipment, and 66.7% on buying back its own shares.
