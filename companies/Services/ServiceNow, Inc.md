**How large is ServiceNow, Inc., and how has it grown?** It sold $13.3 billion in FY2025, the 251st most of the 488 companies with a revenue for the year, and the 31st of 75 in [Services](../Services.md). Its sales rose 1220.6% from FY2015 to FY2025, 29.4% a year.

**Does ServiceNow, Inc. make money, and how much of what it sells does it keep?** It earned $1.7 billion in FY2025, 13.2% of what it sold; the median company of its sector kept 16.3%. It lost money in 8 of its 16 years in the book (FY2010, FY2012, FY2013 and 5 more).

**Where does ServiceNow, Inc.'s cash go?** From FY2016 to FY2025 its operations brought in $22.7 billion; it spent 20% of that on plant and equipment, and 13.8% on buying back its own shares.
