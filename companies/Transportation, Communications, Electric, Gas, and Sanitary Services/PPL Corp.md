PPL Corp, as it files with the SEC (CIK 0000922224): its fiscal years and quarters from its XBRL filings, FY2009 to FY2026 (FY2026 through Q1). Dollars are in millions; EPS in dollars a share. Capex and buybacks are cash paid out, so negative. EPS diluted is as filed, a share as it was when filed; EPS adjusted is a share as it is now, each split since divided out. A quarter the filings give only as a running total is the total less the quarters before it; Q4 is the year less the first three.

**How large is PPL Corp, and how has it grown?** It sold $9.2 billion in FY2025, the 303rd most of the 488 companies with a revenue for the year, and the 50th of 59 in [Transportation, Communications, Electric, Gas, and Sanitary Services](../Transportation,%20Communications,%20Electric,%20Gas,%20and%20Sanitary%20Services.md). Its sales rose 19.6% from FY2015 to FY2025, 1.8% a year.

**Does PPL Corp make money, and how much of what it sells does it keep?** It earned $1.2 billion in FY2025, 12.9% of what it sold; the median company of its sector kept 12.9%. It lost money in 1 of its 17 years in the book (FY2021).

**Where does PPL Corp's cash go?** From FY2016 to FY2025 its operations brought in $24.1 billion; it spent 112.8% of that on plant and equipment, and 4.2% on buying back its own shares.
